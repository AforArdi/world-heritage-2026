Eligibility & Application

Selection is strictly based on the online application. Ensure you meet all mandatory criteria below before applying.

Mandatory Criteria

  • ✓
    Age: 23 to 32 years (born between 1994 and 2003 inclusive).
  • ✓
    Language: Proficiency in English (the Forum is exclusively in English).
  • ✓
    Availability: Full availability for the entire duration of the Forum.
  • ✓
    Engagement: Demonstrated involvement in World Heritage activities.
  • ✓
    Motivation: Strong interest in youth, communities, and education.

Professional Profile

Applicants must be actively engaged in one or more of the following areas:

  • Education and teaching in formal learning settings.
  • World Heritage site management.
  • Research and innovation related to World Heritage.
  • Work within NGOs or community-level associations focused on World Heritage.
*Selection also prioritizes balanced geographical representation, gender equality, diversity of backgrounds, and potential for post-Forum impact.
